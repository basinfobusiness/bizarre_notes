Christianity

We believe Christian worship should be directed to the Creator, following the teachings we attribute to Yeshua ben Yosef, the Messiah, whom we identify as Hebrew and from Nazri. We question later doctrines and religious terminology that we see as departing from that message.

Islam

Islam is an Abrahamic religion, like Christianity, with Prophet Mohammed as its central prophet and leader. Muslims regard him as the final prophet. We understand Allah (the only one to be worshipped; in our interpretation, “Al” means “One” and “Ilah” means “Only”) as the Creator worshipped in Islam. We believe the Quran's teachings refer to Allah as the Creator, not as one god among many, and that no one should change the holy teachings of scripture to fit worldly trends.

Traditionalist

Traditional and Indigenous religions are many distinct ways of understanding the sacred, rather than one unified faith. Depending on the tradition, worldviews may emphasize relationships with ancestors, spirits, land, community, and inherited customs, often passed on through ritual and oral teaching.
