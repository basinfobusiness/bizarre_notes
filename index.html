The Unexpected Origin of Bizarre

According to language etymologists at Vocabulary.com, the English language borrowed the word from French in the 1600s. The French got it from the Italian bizzarro (meaning "capricious" or "eccentric"), which in turn likely came from the Spanish word bizarro, meaning "bold" or "brave like a knight."
